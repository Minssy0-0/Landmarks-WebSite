About Turin

Turin is a refined and elegant city in northern Italy, surrounded by the Alps. It was Italy’s first capital and has wide boulevards, grand squares, and baroque architecture. Turin is known for its café culture, chocolate, and the Shroud of Turin. It also plays a major role in Italy’s automotive industry, especially through FIAT.
