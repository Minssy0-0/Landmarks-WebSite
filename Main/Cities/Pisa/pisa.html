About Pisa

Pisa is best known worldwide for the Leaning Tower, located in the Piazza dei Miracoli, along with its cathedral and baptistery. While many visitors come just for the tower, Pisa is also a historic university town with a calm, youthful atmosphere. The Arno River runs through the city, and Pisa offers a quieter experience compared to larger tourist cities.
