About Verona

Verona is a historic city with a romantic reputation, made famous by Shakespeare’s Romeo and Juliet. The city has a well-preserved Roman amphitheater, still used for concerts and opera today. Verona combines ancient history with charming streets, piazzas, and cafés. It’s calmer than big cities like Milan or Rome, making it pleasant to explore.
