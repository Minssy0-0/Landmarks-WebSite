About Rome

Rome is Italy’s capital and feels like an open-air museum. The city is filled with ancient history, from the Colosseum and Roman Forum to countless ruins scattered through everyday streets. It’s also the center of the Catholic Church, with Vatican City, St. Peter’s Basilica, and the Sistine Chapel. Beyond history, Rome has busy plazas, great food, and a lively atmosphere that blends past and present.
