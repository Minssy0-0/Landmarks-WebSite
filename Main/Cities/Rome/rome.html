About Rome

Rome, the capital of Italy, is one of the most historically rich and culturally significant cities in the world. Founded in 753 BC, the city was once the heart of the Roman Empire, whose influence shaped laws, architecture, language, and governance across much of Europe and beyond. Today, Rome is a living museum where ancient ruins such as the Colosseum, Roman Forum, and Pantheon coexist with Renaissance masterpieces, Baroque fountains, and vibrant modern life. Its iconic piazzas, narrow cobblestone streets, and grand basilicas create an atmosphere found nowhere else in the world.
